2. Write the YDSE fringe-width formula.

β = λD/d

|A − λI| = 0

4. Write the energy formula for a particle in a box.

Eₙ = n²h² / 8mL²

d sinθ = nλ

6. What is the condition for consistency of AX = B?

rank(A) = rank(A|B)

7. What is population inversion?

Population inversion is the condition in which the population of the relevant upper laser level is greater than that of the lower level.

S = E × H
