Q5. What is displacement?

Answer: Displacement is the shortest directed distance between the initial and final positions of an object.

Q6. What is a mole?

Q7. What is a primary key?

3️⃣ Short Answer Questions — 2 Marks

Q8. Write any two differences between distance and displacement.

Answer:
1. Distance is a scalar quantity, while displacement is a vector quantity.

2. Distance represents the total path travelled, while displacement represents the directed change in position.

Q9. Write two important properties of a chemical bond.

Answer:
A chemical bond is an attractive interaction that holds atoms or ions together. Bond formation generally results in a more stable electronic arrangement.

Q10. What is the difference between a list and a dictionary in Python?

Answer:
A list stores elements in an ordered sequence using indexes, whereas a dictionary stores data as key-value pairs.

4️⃣ Numerical Practice

Q11. A body starts from rest and moves with an acceleration of 2 m/s² for 5 seconds. Find its final velocity.

Solution:
Given:
u = 0 m/s
a = 2 m/s²
t = 5 s

Formula:
v = u + at

v = 0 + (2 × 5)

v = 10 m/s

Q12. Find the kinetic energy of a 5 kg body moving with a velocity of 4 m/s.

Solution:
Given:
m = 5 kg
v = 4 m/s

K.E. = ½mv²

= ½ × 5 × 4²
= 2.5 × 16
= 40 J

5️⃣ Long Answer Practice

Q13. State and explain Newton's three laws of motion with suitable examples.

Answer:
First Law: An object remains at rest or in uniform motion in a straight line unless acted upon by an external unbalanced force.

Second Law: The rate of change of momentum is related to the applied force. For constant mass:

F = ma

Third Law: For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.

Q14. Explain the mole concept and write its importance in chemical calculations.

Answer:
A mole is a unit for measuring the amount of substance. One mole contains approximately 6.022 × 10²³ elementary particles.

The mole concept helps in relating the mass of a substance to the number of particles and is widely used in stoichiometric calculations.

Q15. Explain Python conditional statements with an example.

Answer:
Conditional statements allow a program to execute different blocks depending on a condition.

Example: 
marks = 60

if marks >= 40:
    print("Pass")
else:
    print("Fail")
