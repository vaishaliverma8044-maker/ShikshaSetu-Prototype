📐 Mathematics Practice

Question 1 1 Mark
If A = {1,2,3} and B = {2,4,6}, which relation represents "double of" from A to B?
A) {(1,2),(2,4),(3,6)}
B) {(2,1),(4,2),(6,3)}
C) {(1,1),(2,2),(3,3)}
D) {(1,3),(2,4),(3,5)}
Answer: A) {(1,2),(2,4),(3,6)}
Question 2 2 Marks
Find the inverse of the matrix A = [[1,2],[3,4]].
Answer: det(A) = -2. Therefore, A⁻¹ = 1/(-2) [[4,-2],[-3,1]] = [[-2,1],[3/2,-1/2]].
Question 3 3 Marks
Differentiate y = x³ + 5x² - 7x + 4.
Answer: dy/dx = 3x² + 10x - 7.
Question 4 4 Marks
Evaluate: ∫ (2x + 3) dx.
Answer: ∫(2x+3)dx = x² + 3x + C.
Question 5 4 Marks
Find the probability of getting exactly two heads when three coins are tossed simultaneously.
Answer: Favourable outcomes = HHT, HTH, THH = 3. Total outcomes = 8. Probability = 3/8.

⚛️ Physics Practice

Question 1 1 Mark
What is the SI unit of electric charge?
Answer: Coulomb (C).
Question 2 2 Marks
State Coulomb's law.
Answer: The electrostatic force between two point charges is directly proportional to the product of their charges and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them. F = k q₁q₂/r².
Question 3 3 Marks
State Ohm's law and write its mathematical expression.
Answer: At constant temperature, current through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across it. V = IR.
Question 4 4 Marks
Write Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction.
Answer: The induced emf is equal to the negative rate of change of magnetic flux through the circuit. ε = -dΦ/dt.
Question 5 4 Marks
What is the principle of a transformer?
Answer: A transformer works on mutual electromagnetic induction. It transfers electrical energy from one circuit to another through changing magnetic flux.

🧪 Chemistry Practice

Question 1 1 Mark
What is molarity?
Answer: Molarity is the number of moles of solute present in one litre of solution. M = moles of solute / volume of solution in litres.
Question 2 2 Marks
Define electrochemical cell.
Answer: An electrochemical cell is a device in which chemical energy and electrical energy are converted into each other.
Question 3 3 Marks
What is the rate constant of a chemical reaction?
Answer: The rate constant is the proportionality constant in the rate equation of a reaction at a particular temperature.
Question 4 4 Marks
What are coordination compounds? Give one example.
Answer: Coordination compounds contain a central metal atom or ion surrounded by ligands. Example: [Cu(NH₃)₄]SO₄.
Question 5 4 Marks
Differentiate between aldehydes and ketones.
Answer: Aldehydes contain the -CHO group, whereas ketones contain the carbonyl group between two carbon groups.

💻 Computer / IP Practice

Question 1 1 Mark
Which SQL command is used to retrieve data from a table?
A) INSERT
B) SELECT
C) DELETE
D) UPDATE
Answer: B) SELECT
Question 2 2 Marks
What is a DataFrame in Pandas?
Answer: A DataFrame is a two-dimensional labelled data structure in Pandas, similar to a table.
Question 3 3 Marks
Write an SQL query to display all records from a table named Student.
Answer: SELECT * FROM Student;
Question 4 4 Marks
What is the purpose of Matplotlib in Python?
Answer: Matplotlib is used to create graphs, charts and other visualizations from data.
Question 5 4 Marks
What is a computer network? Name any two types of networks.
Answer: A computer network is a group of connected computers/devices that communicate and share resources. Examples: LAN and WAN.

📖 English Practice

Question 1 1 Mark
Choose the correct form: She ____ to school every day.
A) go
B) goes
C) going
D) gone
Answer: B) goes
Question 2 2 Marks
What is the main purpose of a formal letter?
Answer: A formal letter is used for official or professional communication.
Question 3 3 Marks
Change into passive voice: "The teacher praised the student."
Answer: "The student was praised by the teacher."
Question 4 4 Marks
Write four important features of a good notice.
Answer: A good notice should have a clear heading, date, concise information and the issuing authority/signature.

🧬 Biology Practice

Question 1 1 Mark
What is DNA?
Answer: DNA is deoxyribonucleic acid, the genetic material in most organisms.
Question 2 2 Marks
What is fertilisation?
Answer: Fertilisation is the fusion of male and female gametes to form a zygote.
Question 3 3 Marks
What is genetic inheritance?
Answer: Genetic inheritance is the transmission of genetic information from parents to offspring.
Question 4 4 Marks
What is biotechnology?
Answer: Biotechnology is the application of biological organisms, cells or processes to develop useful products and technologies.
